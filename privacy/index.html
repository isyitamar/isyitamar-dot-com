Privacy Policy & Storage Choices

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Who operates this website

ISY ITAMAR operates this website and determines how information submitted through it is used. For privacy questions, access or correction requests, contact isyitamar2@gmail.com or Discord isy_itamar.

Information we collect and why

  • Accounts: name or nickname, email, Discord username and an account identifier. Google sign-in may also supply a display name and profile photo. Authentication credentials are processed by Firebase Authentication; passwords are not included in order emails.
  • Contact requests: name, email, optional Discord details, subject and message, to answer your request and discuss services.
  • Orders: product, plan, quantity, requested website name, discount, total, customer details, timestamps and payment-report status, to arrange and deliver the service and keep transaction records.
  • Technical information: our hosting, authentication and form providers may process IP addresses, browser/device details and security logs to deliver and protect their services.

Providing personal information is voluntary. Required fields are needed for the requested account, contact or order service; without them that feature may not work. You can browse public pages without creating an account. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive information in messages.

Orders and payments

Clicking “I sent the money” sends an order notification through our form provider to the site operator's inbox. It is a customer report, not confirmation that payment was received. Payment is checked manually. The demo sends a clearly marked test notification with the name “test”; its QR does not collect real money. The live checkout shows a QR link to bit for a manual transfer. bit handles the transfer under its own policy. Order drafts are stored in your browser; when you report payment, the order details and your contact details are sent through Formspree to the operator. Prices, order details and receipt of funds are checked manually before any service is confirmed. A requested domain is a preference only: this version does not check, reserve or purchase it and does not charge a domain fee.

Cookies and other device storage

Cookies are small browser records. This website also uses local storage, session storage and Firebase-managed browser storage. These are not all cookies, and they have different lifetimes.

  • Essential functionality: cart contents, checkout attempts, order state, sign-in session, security and your selected accessibility settings. Cart and profile drafts are keyed to the current account or a separate demo key on this browser. Local records remain until cleared or replaced; cart items are removed after a successful payment report.
  • Privacy choice: the isy_privacy cookie lasts up to six months; the isy-privacy-choice local record remains until changed or browser storage is cleared. It records your choice, version and time.
  • Optional remembered sign-in: enabled only if you choose “OK”. Choose “I don’t agree” to use essential storage only; authentication then uses session persistence. You can change this under Cookie settings; changing your choice does not delete an account or previously submitted messages.
  • Accessibility preferences: stored on this device to retain adjustments you explicitly select. Reset them in Accessibility settings.

No advertising pixels or analytics tracking are installed by this version. External service requests may still occur to load fonts, images, sign-in services or public platform information. Refusing remembered sign-in does not block essential service requests. Browsers can clear or block storage; doing so may sign you out and remove the local cart.

Optional read-aloud

Read-aloud starts only when you activate it. It uses your browser’s speech service to read page text, which may include information currently visible in your account or checkout. Voice processing may be local or provided by your browser/operating-system service, depending on your device and selected voice. Stop reading with the same control.

Providers and recipients

Information is shared as needed with Google/Firebase for authentication and configured profile/order storage, Formspree for contact and order notifications, the operator's email provider for received messages, and the hosting/CDN provider for delivery and security. The demo uses the existing Formspree endpoint configured by the operator. External font, image and platform services may receive technical request data when their content loads. Discord, Kick, Steam, YouTube, TikTok and payment links take you to independently operated services. Their practices are governed by their own policies.

Providers may process data outside your country, including outside Israel, according to their arrangements and applicable requirements. We do not sell your personal information or use the submitted details for unsolicited marketing in this implementation. Information may be disclosed when legally required or reasonably necessary to address fraud, security incidents or legal claims.

Retention and security

Browser records persist as described above. Account records remain until deletion is requested and completed; messages, order records and provider logs are retained as necessary to handle requests, provide services, resolve disputes and meet applicable recordkeeping requirements. Exact provider retention periods depend on the configured services. No automatic deletion schedule for server orders or inbox messages is implemented in this version. Reasonable access controls and authenticated account services are used, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and requests

You may contact us to request access to or correction of information about you, and to request deletion where applicable. We may need to verify your identity. Some records may need to be retained for legal obligations or unresolved transactions; we will explain applicable limits. Clearing browser storage does not delete server records or emails. Do not provide information about someone else without appropriate permission.

Changes

This policy describes this version's actual features. Material changes to data uses or service providers should be reflected here before those changes go live, with renewed choices where required. No future policy update is automatic. Check the date above and contact us with questions.
