Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 16 October 2026

ISY ITAMAR aims to make its website usable by people with different abilities and assistive technologies. Our improvement target is WCAG 2.2 Level AA, taking relevant Israeli accessibility requirements into account. This is a target, not a certification or a claim of full conformance.

Features available

  • An accessibility panel with text enlargement, spacing, dark/light contrast, grayscale, saturation and brightness controls.
  • Reading ruler and focus band, link and heading highlights, line-height adjustment and browser-based page read-aloud.
  • A user-selected reduced-motion mode for visual effects.
  • Visible keyboard focus, a skip-to-content link and keyboard-operable panel controls. Click the accessibility button again, or press Escape inside the panel, to close it. Focus returns to its trigger.
  • Normal copying, selection and browser keyboard shortcuts are enabled.

The reading ruler follows the pointer; Alt + Up/Down moves it with the keyboard. Adjustments are saved on your device. Reset settings restores the default appearance. Browser zoom can also be used.

Known limitations and evaluation

This website contains cinematic 3D transitions, animated backgrounds, product previews and third-party services. The panel is a convenience tool and does not make every component accessible by itself. Visual effects, small-screen layouts, external login/payment experiences and some image descriptions may still require improvements. The complete site has not received an independent accessibility audit, a full keyboard and screen-reader evaluation or a WCAG conformance assessment. Automated/source checks cover selected controls only. We do not claim full compliance or exemption.

Ongoing updates

We intend to review accessibility as features change, address reported barriers and update this statement to reflect completed work and remaining limitations. Third-party services are operated independently; tell us if they prevent you from using a feature so we can discuss another way to help.
